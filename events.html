What to expect

new here? here's what to expect

Parent nights and speakers

An expert shares the research, and there's time for your questions. You'll leave with ideas you can try at home that same week. Past speakers include psychologist Jean Twenge.

Device-free gatherings

Play, food, games and conversation, with the phones put away. Fun for the whole family, and usually the best thing on the calendar.

Coffees and small groups

A handful of parents around a table, talking honestly about what's working and what isn't. No agenda, no judgment.

Coalition calls and meetups

For community leads and anyone curious about leading. We meet monthly on a call and in person several times a year.
