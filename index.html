For group leaders

already leading a group?

Maybe you're crushing it in your district. Maybe you're frustrated with how things are going. Maybe it's a little bit of both. Either way, join our support network of parents doing the hard work in their Massachusetts communities. Share wins, and get suggestions when you hit a wall. We lift each other up. That's our biggest flex.

Our coalition includes independent groups and chapters of national organizations, like Wait Until 8th, Schools Beyond Screens and The Balance Project. Either way, you'll be connected with people in Massachusetts and be part of a collective action network that gets results.
