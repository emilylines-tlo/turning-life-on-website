A statewide movement rooted in local action

Turning Life On is a Massachusetts 501(c)(3) that helps local leaders start and sustain grassroots digital-wellness movements, with playbooks, funding, networking, and expert resources.

We work alongside parents, educators, school leaders, and professionals across Massachusetts and beyond to shift the norms around technology, so children grow up with room to learn, play, and connect in the real world.
