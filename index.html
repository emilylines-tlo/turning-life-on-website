A statewide movement rooted in local action

Turning Life On is a 501(c)(3) and Massachusetts-based coalition of parents and caregivers interested in, or already leading, local efforts to shift the norms around technology. Whether you are a seasoned advocate, just getting started, or simply looking for information, we are here for you.

We support that work with playbooks, funding, networking, and expert resources. Together, we are building room for kids to learn, play, and connect in the real world.

What we're working on

The issues

Slow Tech

Delaying harmful tech and resisting addictive design features. We support families, communities, and schools with resources and community pacts to delay smartphones until 9th grade and social media until 16.

Explore resources

Distraction Free Schools and Intentional EdTech

Our kids deserve a learning environment that is free of distraction, safe, and effective. We work with parents, educators, and lawmakers to get there.

Take action

Reconnecting our communities

Through screen-free and play-based events and educational opportunities. Taking tech away does not solve the problem on its own — we need to recreate the spaces and moments that bring people together without a device in the way.

How we get there

Supporting our Community Leads

Networking

Monthly coalition calls, info sessions, and in-person meetups connect our leads to a statewide network of advocates. They share what works, build relationships, and back each other up — so one community's breakthrough becomes everyone's.

Join a coalition call

Partnerships

We work alongside local and national organizations, legislators, the Attorney General's Office, and the Governor's Office to match local action to state policy. Strong ties to leading professionals let us co-create resources and connect those experts directly to communities.

Funding

As a 501(c)(3), we continually grow our base of annual philanthropic support so we can award grants to communities — money that lets local leaders fund their own initiatives.

Why communities turn to us

We are the movement

Turning Life On didn't show up yesterday. Since 2017 we have grown into a statewide network of more than a dozen Massachusetts communities.

Nearly a decade in, we have built what cannot be replicated overnight:

  • Direct lines to policymakers — the Massachusetts Attorney General's Office, the State House, and the Governor's Office.
  • Leadership with a track record — our board has chaired the Distraction Free Schools Policy Project and sits on the board of Smartphone Free Childhood U.S.
  • National partnerships — Fairplay, The Anxious Generation, the Four Norms Coalition, and Culture Reframed bring the latest research and strategy to our local leads.
  • Playbooks built from Massachusetts experience — tested in real MA districts, not adapted from somewhere else.
  • Funding that goes straight to the ground — every dollar we have raised has supported local groups directly, and we are pursuing more to reach more communities.
  • An experienced in-state network — our community leads understand the particular challenges and opportunities in Massachusetts, and how to make the most of them.

A statewide coalition moves faster. When one Massachusetts community solves a problem, every other community benefits. If you are in Massachusetts and ready to make a difference, we are the coalition built to go the distance with you.
