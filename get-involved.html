leading the way in your community

For advocates (leading change in your community and district). We exist so you don't have to reinvent the wheel. Use our resources to get started and keep going.

And you never have to do this alone. When a school committee meeting goes your way, we're your cheerleaders. When it doesn't, we're an ear to listen, a hand to pick you up and a team to brainstorm what's next. On the hard days, we'll remind you that you're making a difference. Working alongside so many smart, dedicated parents is what keeps all of us going.
