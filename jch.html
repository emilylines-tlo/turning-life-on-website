About the speaker

dr. jared cooney horvath

Dr. Jared Cooney Horvath is a cognitive neuroscientist and educator who has conducted research and taught at Harvard University, Harvard Medical School and the University of Melbourne, and has worked with more than 1,000 schools around the world. He is the founder of LME Global and the creator of The Learning Blueprint.

His New York Times bestseller The Digital Delusion: How Classroom Technology Harms Our Kids' Learning, and How to Help Them Thrive Again has helped spark a national movement to rethink screens in schools.

In January 2026 he testified before the U.S. Senate Committee on Commerce, Science and Transportation at its hearing on the impact of technology on America's youth. His work has been featured by CNN, the BBC, The Atlantic, The New Yorker and C-SPAN.

Sources: Penguin Random House, The Digital Delusion, published 18 August 2026; U.S. Senate Committee on Commerce, Science and Transportation, written testimony of Dr. Jared Cooney Horvath, “Plugged Out: Examining the Impact of Technology on America's Youth,” 15 January 2026.
