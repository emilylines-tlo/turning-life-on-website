Who we are

parents like you, since 2017

Turning Life On started in Concord with a few parents and a simple promise to hold off on smartphones together. Today we're a coalition of parent-led groups in more than two dozen Massachusetts communities, helping families keep tech in check at home, at school and in their communities. We didn't parachute into Massachusetts. We grew up here.

Watch our story on WBZ-TV Join us
What we believe

a continuum, not a switch

We're not anti-tech. We help families, schools and communities move toward a healthier, more intentional, age-appropriate relationship with technology, wherever they're starting from. Every step toward balance counts.

Where we stand on tech

“It's less about turning screens off. TLO is about turning on relationships, curiosity, senses, support, and activism.”

Jean Rogers, M.S.Ed., Director, Screen Time Action Network at Fairplay
Why parents trust us

nearly a decade of groundwork

  • We know Massachusetts. Our playbooks were built and tested in real Massachusetts districts, and our leaders have started groups in Arlington, Concord, Lexington and the Nashoba Valley.
  • We're at the table. We work with the Attorney General's Office, the State House and the Governor's Office, and with national partners like Fairplay, The Anxious Generation and Four Norms.
  • We put money where it matters. We raise funds and give them to local parent groups for speakers, screenings and events.
Our team

led by parents who do this work in their own Massachusetts communities, too

Adrienne Principe

Founder and President · Concord

A certified Child and Teen Coach who serves on the Massachusetts Distraction Free Schools Policy Project and was a founding member of a national working group on screens in schools.

Read more

A certified Child and Teen Coach, Adrienne blends the latest research on child development with real-world parenting wisdom to offer practical, values-based strategies for healthy tech use. She co-founded the Concord Promise, serves on the MA Distraction Free Schools Policy Project, is a founding member of the Children's Screen Time Action Network's Screens in Schools Working Group, and sits on the Emerson Health Foundation Board of Directors. Adrienne lives in Concord with her husband, their four busy and very different kids, and their dog, Gus.

Emily Lines

Vice President · Concord

Brings a background in finance, nonprofit leadership and strategic consulting to helping parents and schools make intentional tech decisions.

Read more

Emily founded the Intentional Tech Coalition in Concord and is a dedicated advocate for community-based partnerships that help parents, caregivers and schools make intentional, developmentally aligned technology decisions. She's deeply committed to protecting childhood, supporting mental health, and strengthening family and community connections. Emily holds a master's degree in Animals and Public Policy and brings a background in finance, nonprofit leadership and strategic consulting. She lives in Concord with her husband, children, and rescued pit bulls.

Local group: Intentional Tech Coalition

Wendi Hoffer

Director of Community Engagement · Lexington

Has led a nonprofit and works for another, and builds the network that helps community leads push for change with their school districts.

Read more

Wendi founded Lex Kids Be Kids and is a passionate advocate for a more human, less digital childhood. At Turning Life On, she builds the framework that connects community leads so they can support one another, a network that has proven especially valuable when advocating for change with school districts. Wendi works for a separate nonprofit and has led one, and she channels that experience into meaningful change for families and vulnerable communities. She lives in Lexington with her husband and three diversely-abled children.

Local group: Lex Kids Be Kids

Jeff Miller

Treasurer and AI Specialist · Arlington

A business executive who has served on 10 corporate boards and now sits on the board of Smartphone Free Childhood U.S.

Read more

Jeff founded Tech-Wise Arlington, a parent-to-parent guide that helps local families think through smartphones, phone-free schools, classroom tech and AI, including a plain-language guide to AI for parents. He's an advocate for Wait Until 8th and phone-free schools and works to share ideas and resources across communities. Jeff is EVP of Strategy and Acquisition at Acxion Foodservice and has served on 10 corporate boards. He also sits on the boards of Smartphone Free Childhood U.S., the Arlington Libraries Foundation, and the Humanist Chaplaincy at Harvard and MIT. Jeff holds an A.B. in Economics from Harvard and lives in Arlington with his wife, two children, and two dogs.

Local group: Tech-Wise Arlington

Kelsey Magnuson

Director of Development · Bolton

A public health professional with more than 10 years of experience turning community data into programs that improve health.

Read more

Kelsey is a public health professional with more than 10 years of experience translating community data into strategic programming that improves health, including local Youth Risk Behavior Survey data showing growing concern about youth mental health. She is passionate about bringing people together for shared learning and program development. Kelsey first worked with Turning Life On in 2020, developing an educational campaign through website content and a mini podcast series, and as Director of Development she now leads TLO's fundraising through grants and sponsorships. Sheila Pundit

Device-Free Events · Sudbury

A longtime teacher and school administrator who ran a Boston nonprofit writing program for middle schoolers.

Read more

Sheila works with Sudbury for Digital Balance and spent many years as a teacher and administrator in public schools, after-school programs and summer programs.
