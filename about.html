Who we are

We grew up here

Turning Life On is a Massachusetts-based coalition helping communities shift the norms around technology. What sets us apart is where we came from: we didn't parachute into Massachusetts, we grew up here. Since 2017, our grassroots start has become a network of experienced, in-state community leads who know the unique challenges and opportunities in Massachusetts communities and districts because we've lived them.

We back local groups with playbooks, funding, connections and expert support, so more kids get room to learn, play and connect in the real world.

Why communities turn to us

Nearly a decade of groundwork

Here's how our experience supports your work.

  • Direct lines to decision-makers. We work with the Massachusetts Attorney General's Office, the State House and the Governor's Office, and were invited to join Governor Healey for her announcement of new social media protections for kids in May 2026.
  • Leaders who've done this work. Our board includes a founding member of the Children's Screen Time Action Network's Screens in Schools Working Group, a former co-lead of the Distraction Free Schools Policy Project, a founding member and former co-lead of Smartphone Free Childhood U.S., and a Smartphone Free Childhood U.S. board member. Our leaders have founded local groups in Arlington, Concord, Lexington and the Nashoba Valley, and have experience in education, technology, communication and non-profits.
  • National partners. Fairplay, The Anxious Generation, the Four Norms Leadership Circle and Culture Reframed, to name a few, work with us to bring the latest research and strategy to our local leads.
  • Playbooks built here. Tested in real Massachusetts districts, not adapted from somewhere else.
  • Funding for local groups. We raise money so community leads can pay for rooms, speakers and events, and we're dedicated to growing our philanthropic base to support even more communities.
  • A network of experienced leads. People who know the challenges in Massachusetts, and the openings too.

A statewide coalition moves faster. When one community figures something out, every other community benefits.

What we believe

A continuum, not a switch

Technology use exists on a continuum, from constant, compulsive engagement to complete disconnection. Our goal is to help families, schools, and communities move toward a healthier, more intentional, and age-appropriate relationship with tech, regardless of where they are starting from.

We are not anti-tech. We recognize what technology makes possible, and we meet people where they are, supporting any step toward balance.
