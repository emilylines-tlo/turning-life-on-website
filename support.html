Individual donations

what your gift makes possible

Donations fund grants to parent-led communities, so they can bring in speakers, host movie screenings, run device-free gatherings and parent nights, and reach families who wouldn't otherwise hear this conversation. Your gift also supports:

  • Speakers and screenings: experts, authors and films that bring parents together and spark real conversation
  • Events across Massachusetts: rooms, materials and printables for parent nights, coffees and device-free gatherings
  • Free resources: guides, playbooks and model policies any family, school or community can use
  • A statewide network: monthly coalition calls, meetups, and one-on-one support for community leads
  • Advocacy: keeping parents informed and organized when it's time to speak up at the State House
  • $50 covers materials for a parent night.
  • $250 covers the room for a community event.
  • $1,000 helps bring a speaker to a community that couldn't book one on its own.

Gifts are tax deductible.
