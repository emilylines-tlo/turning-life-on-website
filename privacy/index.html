Our promise

our privacy promise

Last updated: October 2026

Turning Life On is a community of parents, and we treat your information the way we'd want ours treated.

What we collect

When you join the movement, sign a pact or petition, register for an event or contact us, we collect the information you give us, such as your name, email address, zip code, community and anything you choose to share in a message. When families sign the community pact, we may also ask for your child's school and grade. We never ask for your child's name.

How we use it

We use your information to keep you updated, connect you with your local community, count pact signatures by community, school and grade, and let you know about events and ways to take action.

Who sees it

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with outside groups or companies. If you join a community, your information is shared with that community's lead only, and every community lead is vetted by Turning Life On. We use a few trusted services to run our work, like Four Norms for sign-ups and email and PayPal for donations. They handle your information only to provide those services to us.

Your choices

You can opt out of our emails at any time, using the link at the bottom of any email or by writing to us. You can also ask us to update or delete your information.

Questions?

Email us at info@turninglifeon.org.
