The community pact

More childhood before the phone. The pact is a promise among families in the same grade to wait on smartphones until high school and social media until 16. Waiting is so much easier when you're not the only family doing it.

The statewide pact is being built on Four Norms. This address is reserved for it, so any link you share now will keep working once it's live.
