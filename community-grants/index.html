Community Grants

Good ideas shouldn't stall over a budget.

Our grants help local groups bring people together: parent nights, speaker events, device-free gatherings, and projects that help families use technology in healthy, intentional, age-appropriate ways.

What we fund

Projects that:

  • Promote healthy tech habits for kids, teens, parents and educators
  • Create hands-free, heads-up experiences and face-to-face connection
  • Help people understand how technology affects kids' social, emotional and cognitive development
  • Offer practical, community-based tools for balancing tech and wellbeing
  • Bring schools, families and communities together

Grants can cover supplies and materials, speaker fees, venue and equipment, and marketing.

How much

Most grants range from $250 to $2,500, depending on the project's scope and impact.

Who can apply

Grants are available to community groups registered on Four Norms as Turning Life On groups. Not registered yet? It's quick, and we'll help you set it up. Start a group

How it works

  1. Download the application, fill it out, and email it to info@turninglifeon.org.
  2. We review applications on a rolling basis and will get back to you within 14 days of receiving yours.
  3. Within 30 days of your event, send us a short summary report.

What we ask in return

Every event we fund helps us raise money for the next one, so we ask grant recipients to:

  • Collect RSVPs through your Turning Life On-connected Four Norms group, so we can show funders the impact
  • Put the Turning Life On logo on flyers, programs and slides
  • Display our event sign, with its donation QR code, at your event
  • Include a line like "This event is made possible through a grant from Turning Life On" in your materials
  • Tag @TurningLifeOn on social media when you can

More ways we can help

If your group is registered on Four Norms under the Turning Life On umbrella, we can help you use our 501(c)(3) status to book free community space. We can also help you apply for event-specific funding from other sources, like local hospitals and community funds.
