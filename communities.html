start one. we'll help

You don't need a big following, a background in policy, or a free evening every week. Most groups start with a handful of parents who are tired of feeling alone with this.

  1. Talk with us.

    Tell us about your community, and we'll help you find a good first step.

    Book a call
  2. Gather a few parents.

    Three to ten is plenty. Our Getting Started playbook includes a letter you can send.

    Getting Started playbook
  3. Pick an easy first step.

    For example, a parent coffee or a device-free event.

    Plan a community conversation
